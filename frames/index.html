What's in the pack

File Size What it marks
Reel safe zones 1080×1920 Red: what the Instagram app covers on a reel. The top 220 px (your name, the audio tag), the bottom 420 px (caption, comment bar) and the right edge (like, comment, share).
Reel grid crop 1080×1920 Dark: what your profile grid hides. By default the grid shows the middle 1080 × 1440 (3:4) of a reel.
Post grid crop 1080×1350 Dark: the 34 px the 3:4 grid trims off each side of a 4:5 post.

How we use them

  1. Put the PNG on the top video track, above everything, for the whole sequence.
  2. Keep every word, face and logo out of the coloured areas.
  3. Switch the track off before you export, or the guide ends up in the video.

They work in any editor that takes a transparent PNG: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut.

The right-edge strip is drawn to clear the buttons. Instagram doesn't publish a number for it.
